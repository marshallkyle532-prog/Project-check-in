Built for project teams.

Project Check-in is designed for teams that need a consistent way to report progress, track delivery, and communicate project health without heavy process overhead.

From internal product work to client delivery, it gives leaders a simple view of what is moving, what is delayed, and what needs follow-up.

  • Shared project visibility across the whole team
  • Faster status reporting for managers and stakeholders
  • Cleaner communication around deadlines and blockers
  • Better alignment between product, design, and delivery
